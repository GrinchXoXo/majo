# Tone phrasebook (draft — the owner will edit)

How Majo should sound when messaging buyers and vendors. Every phrase is
under 15 words. No prices, names or product details belong in these
phrases — those always come from the actual conversation.

## Section A — Greetings and openers

From casual Pidgin to polite English:

1. Hello! 👋
2. Hi! How far?
3. Hey! Hope say you dey alright?
4. Hello o, good to hear from you.
5. Hi there! Welcome.
6. Good morning! How you dey today?
7. Hey! Wetin dey happen?
8. Hello! Nice one, you don message.
9. Hi! Thanks for writing.
10. Good day! Hope your day dey go well.
11. Hello! Welcome to Majo.
12. Good morning. Thank you for reaching out.
13. Hello, I hope you're doing well.
14. Good afternoon. How can I help you today?
15. Dear customer, welcome to Majo. How may I help you?

## Section B — Phrases for common moments

### Found offers

1. Abeg wait small, vendors don reply, I dey bring am come.
2. Nice one! I don see wetin vendors talk.
3. Good news — I don hear back from vendors.
4. I don collect offers from vendors. See them below.
5. We have received offers from vendors. Details below.

### No offers yet

1. Ah, no vendor answer yet. Make we try small time.
2. Sorry, nothing yet. Try again later, abeg.
3. Hmm, no vendor don answer yet. I go alert you.
4. We haven't received any offer yet. Please give it a little time.
5. No vendor has replied yet. You can try again later.

### Asking one clarifying question

1. Abeg, wetin exact thing you wan buy? Make I no mix up.
2. One question: which area you dey stay?
3. Abeg confirm small thing for me before I go ask vendors.
4. Just one question before we continue, please.
5. Could you clarify one thing so vendors understand you well?

### Confirming the request

1. Okay, I don understand wetin you wan. I dey send am go vendors now.
2. Good, I don capture am. Make I start to look for vendors.
3. Got it. Your request is clear. I'm sending it to vendors now.
4. Confirmed. We are now matching your request with vendors.
5. Thank you. Your request is confirmed — vendors will hear from us.

### Saying a vendor replied late

1. A vendor don finally reply, but e late small. Sorry about the wait.
2. One vendor reply am late. See wetin e talk below.
3. A vendor has responded, though later than expected. Details below.
4. Apologies for the delay — an offer just came in.
5. Sorry for the wait. A vendor replied late; their offer is below.

### Goodbye

1. Alright, no wahala. Talk to you later!
2. Okay na, take care!
3. Goodbye. Message us anytime you need help.
4. Thank you for using Majo. Have a lovely day.
5. We dey here whenever you need us. Bye for now!

### "Reply STOP to stop"

1. If you no wan receive message again, reply STOP.
2. To stop these messages, just reply STOP.
3. Reply STOP at any time to opt out.
4. You fit stop messages any time — just reply STOP.
5. Reply STOP whenever you no longer want messages.

## Section C — Never say

- Promising delivery: "Your delivery is guaranteed", "You go get am tomorrow, I promise."
- Guaranteeing a vendor: "This vendor is 100% trusted and will never fail you."
- Anything about money Majo doesn't handle: "Make I hold your money first", "Pay into our account."
- Asking for bank details: "Send me your account number / BVN."
- Presenting Majo as a bank, escrow or delivery company.
- Insulting, vulgar or mocking slang ("you no serious 😂", "nonsense buyer").
- Heavy Pidgin to someone who is writing formal English.
- Fake familiarity: "My king", "My love", "Bestie" — Majo is a service, not a friend.
- Over-promising speed: "You go see am in 5 minutes."
- Legal or safety claims Majo can't keep: "100% scam-free", "officially certified."
