## What Majo stores

- Your WhatsApp phone number
- The messages you send to Majo and receive from Majo
- Your requests (what you want to buy, budget, location)
- Offers from vendors about your request

## Why Majo stores it

To run the service: to understand your request, find vendors who can help,
send offers back to you, and keep a record if something needs checking
later.

## Who sees it

The vendors matched to your request see the request itself — what you
want, roughly where you are, and your budget. They do not see your other
messages or your other data. Majo's operator (the person running the
pilot) can see everything in the system, because they run it.
