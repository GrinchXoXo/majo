# Buyer guide

## WhatsApp invite message (send to a buyer)

> Hi! This is Majo, a WhatsApp service for this campus/community pilot.
> Tell us what you want to buy — for example "I need a used iPhone 13,
> around 650k" — and we ask nearby vendors who has it. You see the offers
> and pick one. It is free to use. Deal directly with the vendor for
> payment and delivery. Reply STOP anytime to stop messages.

## Buyer FAQ

**How do I ask for something?**
Just send Majo a WhatsApp message saying what you want, and optionally a
price. Write like you'd write a friend.

**Will Majo ask me questions?**
Yes, maybe one or two — like your location or your exact budget — to make
sure vendors understand you.

**Is it free?**
Yes. The pilot is free for buyers. There is no Majo fee.

**Who are the vendors?**
Invited vendors from our campus or community, known to the Majo team.
They are not employees of Majo.

**Do I pay Majo?**
No. **During this pilot, all payment goes directly to the vendor.** Majo
never holds your money, never takes a deposit, and never guarantees
delivery.

**What if no vendor replies?**
You will be told there were no offers. You can try again later or change
what you're looking for.

**How do I know an offer is real?**
Vendors reply with availability and price, but Majo cannot guarantee that
a vendor will complete a sale. Meet in safe public places, or pay on
delivery where possible.

**How do I stop messages?**
Reply **STOP** to Majo at any time.

**What happens to my chats?**
Majo stores your phone number, messages, requests and offers to run the
service. See the pilot privacy notice: `docs/pilot/privacy-notice-draft.md`.
